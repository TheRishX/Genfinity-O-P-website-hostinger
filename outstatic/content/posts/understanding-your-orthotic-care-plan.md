## A clearer next step

Starting orthotic care should feel collaborative and understandable. Your evaluation gives the clinical team a chance to learn about your movement, comfort, daily routines, and goals.

## What to expect

Your care plan may include an assessment, measurements or scanning, a fitting appointment, and follow-up adjustments. Each step helps your device support the way you actually move.

If you have questions about fit, comfort, or insurance, bring them to your appointment. The right plan is one that fits both your clinical needs and your life.
